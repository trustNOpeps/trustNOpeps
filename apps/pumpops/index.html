METHOD / ASSUMPTIONS · Hydraulic power = ρgQH; Q converted from m³/h to m³/s. Input power = hydraulic power ÷ (pump efficiency × motor efficiency). Energy = electric power × operating hours. NPV uses a level annual cash saving and end-of-year discounting, with constant tariffs and no tax, inflation, depreciation, deterioration or financing. All efficiencies expressed as fractions in calculations. This is a first-pass screening tool, not a pump selection, design certification or guarantee of savings. Validate duty point against measured motor input and manufacturer pump curves. U.S. DOE pump systems · DOE industry sourcebook.
