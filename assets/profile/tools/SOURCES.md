# Tool icon sources

48 confirmed tools, grouped in eight categories. Icons are locally stored and wrapped in a consistent dark tile. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners; no affiliation or proficiency rating is implied.

- [Skill Icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons): original 16 icons plus MathWorks MATLAB family mark for Simulink; license in ../SKILL-ICONS-LICENSE.txt.
- [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon): Canva, MATLAB, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Premiere Pro.
- [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons): Next.js, Supabase, GitLab, GitHub Pages, Render, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Tinkercad. Legacy tag 12.4.0: Adobe Lightroom, ChatGPT (OpenAI), Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
- [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons): OpenAI Codex (OpenAI family icon), Lovable, Kling AI, Suno, CapCut, Google Antigravity.
- Original text badges: Higgsfield and Google Flow. These are not official logos.

Individual product logos may have trademark restrictions independent of the source license.
