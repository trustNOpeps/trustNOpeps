# Berpikir di Era AI — Nonfiction Book

**Status:** Commercial manuscript in development · 2026

**[Read the public book preview](https://trustnopeps.github.io/trustNOpeps/book/)**

*Berpikir di Era AI: Cara memakai kecerdasan buatan tanpa menyerahkan penilaianmu* is an Indonesian-language nonfiction manuscript by **haammkaki**. Its focus is critical thought and personal judgment while using AI tools.

## Public overview

The book progresses through four connected themes:

1. Understanding the tool and its limitations.
2. The **JEDA** method for slowing down and checking AI-assisted decisions.
3. Maintaining agency and judgment.
4. Guided practice, worksheets and reflection.

The portfolio page shows an original teaser, thematic outline and typographic cover interpretation. It is an introduction to the project, **not** a full PDF preview.
