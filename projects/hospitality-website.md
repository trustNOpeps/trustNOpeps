# Hospitality website
**Pitch prototype · Brand expression & visitor journey**

For a bar website, visitors need a quick sense of the place, what is on the menu, and how to visit. The visual character matters, but it still needs to support those practical questions.

## The brief
Create a bilingual Indonesian/English pitch prototype for a Bali hospitality venue, with menu, story, gallery, reviews, events, and visit information in the proposed scope.

## My contribution
I worked on the visual direction, content structure, menu presentation, and mobile/desktop revisions through an AI-assisted website workflow.

## Decisions behind the interface
- Develop a tropical-blue direction suited to the intended venue character.
- Keep the main brand mark in the header to reduce unnecessary repetition.
- Present product bottles with consistent sizing and backgrounds.
- Make menu and visit information central to the visitor journey.
- Use labelled placeholders where venue details or assets have not been verified.

## Status and limits
This is a pitch prototype, not a claim of a commissioned or launched client website. The proposed sections do not establish that every feature is implemented. Administrative features, events, bilingual behaviour, accessibility, and responsive performance require implementation-level verification before release.

The venue name, unverified reviews, operating details, and third-party imagery are not reproduced here. No booking or business outcomes are claimed.
