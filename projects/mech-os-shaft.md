# MECH.OS — Shaft Studio

[Live application](https://mech-os-shaft-studio-preview.vercel.app/) · [Source and engineering methods](https://github.com/trustNOpeps/Mechlabs/tree/main/experiments/mech-os)

An educational preliminary design workspace for uniform solid round shafts, combining simply supported beam bending, torque, von Mises stress, static yield factor of safety and optional deflection checks. The rotatable mesh is not precision CAD. Studies are stored in the browser; CSV, JSON, STL and print exports are available.

Assumptions: central transverse load, constant simultaneous torque, linear elasticity and isotropic material. No fatigue, keyway, stress concentration, bearing loads, critical speed or manufacturing tolerance assessment. A passing calculation is not design certification.

Verification evidence: [MechLabs Actions](https://github.com/trustNOpeps/Mechlabs/actions). Vercel production metadata was READY during the 27 November audit; deployed commit and latest source should be compared before release. No new browser or physical validation is asserted here.
