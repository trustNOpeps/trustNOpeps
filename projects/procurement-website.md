# Procurement website
**Self-directed prototype · Product structure & interface direction**

A procurement website needs to help a buyer describe what they need. A conventional shop layout is a poor fit when price and availability depend on specifications, quantity, and stock.

## The brief
Develop a company-profile website with a product catalogue and a request-for-quotation journey. The intended audience is a business buyer researching supplies before contacting the supplier.

## My contribution
I worked on the page structure, category organisation, written content, visual direction, and responsive layout revisions through an AI-assisted website-building workflow.

## Decisions behind the interface
- Organise the catalogue into IT & networking, printers & office equipment, electrical & telecommunications, operational facilities, and stationery & filing.
- Give each product a name, brand or series, three key specifications, and an availability statement.
- Use “Add to request” as the intended action. Fixed public prices would imply certainty that the procurement process cannot always provide.
- Keep the request list accessible from the header so visitors can collect several requirements before asking for a quotation.
- Review navigation, images, catalogue pages, and forms across screen sizes rather than treating responsiveness as a catalogue-only concern.

## Status and limits
