# Property company website
**Self-directed prototype · Information architecture & visual direction**

A property developer needs room to introduce its company and present more than one development. A website centred entirely on a single housing project makes that relationship harder to understand.

## The brief
Structure a company website with housing developments presented as a catalogue, supported by clear project information and layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## My contribution
I worked on company-versus-project page structure, catalogue presentation, written content, visual direction, and responsive revisions using AI-assisted website tools.

## Decisions behind the interface
- Introduce the company at the top level and place individual developments within the project catalogue.
- Preserve the supplied logo instead of redrawing or embellishing it.
- Use an appropriate technical drawing to explain the area layout when available.
- Exclude an unsuitable brochure-derived overhead image from the intended presentation.
- Treat image proportions and readability as part of the responsive design brief.
