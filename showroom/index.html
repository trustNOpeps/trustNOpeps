01 / What remains

Not just
clothing.

AFTER WANTING is an ongoing exploration of how people wear their choices, memories and boundaries.

From graphic-heavy statements to quieter textures, each volume has its own atmosphere. What connects them is a question: what stays after the wanting fades?

THE ARCHIVE.

Digital garment mockups based on the original AFTER WANTING collection studies. These are concept visuals, not photographs of manufactured items.

02 / THE IDEA
Desire is loud.
Choice is ours.

Some pieces speak in contradictions. Others leave room for silence. The collection names invite us to notice what we carry into a room and what we choose to leave behind.

03 / Behind the clothes

A studio of
iterations.

These seven capsules are design concepts. Product mockups are exploratory illustrations; they are not proof of physical samples, fit testing, fabric sourcing or released stock.
